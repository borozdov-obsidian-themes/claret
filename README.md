# Borozdov Claret

A theme from the Borozdov collection. Two faces — light **Sundown**, a golden-hour
editorial dashboard on warm parchment, and dark **Cellar**, the same desk in a wine-dark
room. White cards that lift by contrast, whispering headlines and one midnight wine for
what you act on.

![Borozdov Claret in light mode](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/borozdov-obsidian-themes/claret/main/screenshots/light.png)

![Borozdov Claret in dark mode](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/borozdov-obsidian-themes/claret/main/screenshots/dark.png)

## Principles

- **Parchment, never white.** The canvas is warm parchment; white is reserved for cards
  that need to lift — callouts, tables, panels — and they lift by contrast alone, without
  shadows.
- **Headlines whisper.** The title and every heading at weight 500, tracked in tighter as
  they grow; the platform's own sans at 400 for the text. Nothing shouts at 700.
- **One midnight wine.** A warm near-black maroon fills the main button, a checked task, a
  toggle and the open file. Royal violet carries links; lilac mist washes tags, secondary
  buttons and the highlighter.
- **One dark band.** Code blocks sit in deep lagoon with pale text — the only dark surface
  on the light face, and a distinct teal well on the dark one.

## Features

- Light and dark modes, following Settings → Appearance → Base color scheme
- Callouts as white cards with 16px corners and the title in the type's colour
- Tables as white cards with a parchment header band
- Tags and property values as lilac pills with ink text
- Quiet editing: no focus ring around the note, its title or form fields while you type;
  property names read as labels, not boxed fields
- Text colours meet WCAG contrast on both faces
- The phone layout keeps the same colours and shapes
- No embedded fonts, so the theme stays around 12 KB
- No `!important`: every rule can be overridden with a CSS snippet

## Installation

**From the community directory:** Settings → Appearance → Themes → Manage, search for
**Borozdov Claret**, then **Install and use**.

**By hand:** download `manifest.json` and `theme.css` from the
[latest release](https://github.com/borozdov-obsidian-themes/claret/releases/latest) into
`<vault>/.obsidian/themes/Borozdov Claret/`, then choose Borozdov Claret under
Settings → Appearance → Themes.
